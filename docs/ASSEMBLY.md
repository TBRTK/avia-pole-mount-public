# V1.2 assembly

**Geometrically checked prototype — physical validation pending.** Dimensions are mm. Read the [hardware list](HARDWARE.md). No approved tightening torque is specified. Trial with a dummy sensor/load before service.

1. Clean and measure the trial prints. Remove support/elephant foot from bores, nut seats, cone flanks and four 0.4 mm hinge gaps. Check usable smooth shoulder length, transitions and real hardware dimensions.
2. While the front clamp is loose and its pole groove is accessible, insert **two DIN934 M5 cone nuts from the open pole groove**. Insert the other two DIN934 M5 nuts in the closing-side front pockets. The pole must not block this operation.
3. Interleave five hinge lugs. Put one **6.4×12×0.5 shim** on the pin, then insert the pin **upward from below**. Fit the upper 6.4×12×0.5 shim, then **5.3×10×1 retaining washer**, then norelem 07214-105 locknut. The retaining washer seats against the steel shoulder: the nut must stop on this metal stack, not squeeze plastic lugs. Confirm free rotation, positive endplay and actual thread engagement through the polyamide insert. No sleeve is used.
4. **Remove both closing bolts and their washers before opening.** Rotate only the rear to 100° and install around the Ø35 pole from the side. Front stays fixed. Close and fit **two M5×30 bolts with one 5.3×10×1 washer each**. Tighten evenly only after checking the pole is gripped before printed surfaces bottom. No torque is prescribed.
5. Slide the bracket along global +X over the cone. Fit **two M5×20 bolts with one 5.3×10×1 washer each** in the recesses. Seat all three 25° flanks **before mounting the sensor**, using a straight 4 mm hex tool. Maintain positive outer gap; do not pull exterior faces closed.
6. Fit Avia with **four M3×10 bolts and four 3.2×7×0.5 washers**. The preserved 7 mm plate gives nominal 2.5 mm engagement. Check real full threads, chamfers/runout and Livox's actual permitted penetration. A stated 4 mm sensor thread depth is not a separately tolerance-qualified safe penetration limit.
7. Check actual cable, retention, witness marks, positive cone gaps and hinge endplay/coverage. Inspect the lower lug near M3 access, lug/rib roots, shear tongue and nut seats. Complete [physical validation](LIMITATIONS.md) before sensor use.

## Access and retention

Upper cone bolt: straight access before sensor fitting; afterwards the straight path intersects the tilted sensor. The checked alternative is a **4 mm ball-end hex at 25° from below**, inside a Ø6 envelope. Confirm the real tool fits. Hinge: 3 mm hex below and AF8 socket above (modelled OD13, AF8.3 cavity and Ø6 through-channel for projecting thread).

Nominal pin stack bottom to top: head → lower 6.4 shim → 48 mm lug envelope → upper 6.4 shim → 5.3 retaining washer on shoulder → locknut. Total nominal endplay 1.0 mm; measured acceptance allocation 0.70–1.55 mm. Do not remove this freedom by crushing plastic.
