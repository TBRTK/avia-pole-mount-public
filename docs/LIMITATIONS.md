# Limitations and physical validation

**Geometrically checked prototype — physical validation pending.** PETG is provisional. CAD clearance, valid solids, sampled motion and calculated loads do not establish strength, lasting freedom from play, material approval or safe working load. No tightening torque is specified. Four-bolt clamp capacity assumptions do not apply to V1.2.

Before actual sensor use, document:

- Pole OD/wall, assembled mass/CG, print dimensions, nut/washer fits, shoulder runouts and actual full-thread/polyamide engagement.
- Contact marks on three cone flanks, retained positive outer gap/bottom reserve, draw-in travel and preload retention.
- Grip/slip, backlash, angle stability and elastic stiffness under dummy loads in both directions and on a tilted pole.
- Two bolts gripping before hard stops; actual nut/washer alignment and local deformation.
- Hinge retention, free rotation, endplay, repeated cycles and metal/plastic bearing wear.
- Warm loaded holds (suggested 24/72-hour observation stages), temperature history, creep, vibration, retained grip and angle. These are proposed tests, not completed results or a qualification standard.
- Cracks/delamination at lower lug near M3 access, roots, tongue and nut seats. Approx. 2.15 mm cone-nut edge ligament remains a concern. Local hinge bore-to-outer/access-surface minimum approx. 3.610 mm is geometry, not a material allowable.
- Actual cable bend/routing, FOV, purchased-tool access and Livox screw penetration/assembly limits.

113 discrete opening positions do not prove a complete continuous collision-free angular sweep. Animation is not further verification. Printing distortion, flank angle/flatness errors, material conditioning and temperature are unqualified. A changed material, diameter, parameter or hardware stack is a new unqualified configuration.
